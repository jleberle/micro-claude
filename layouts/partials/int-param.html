{{/* A non-negative integer from a micro.blog backend setting, or a fallback. {{ $n := partial "int-param.html" (dict "raw" .Site.Params.home_books_count "fallback" 5) }} Expects a dict: "raw" (the param, any type, may be missing) and "fallback" (the hardcoded default, mirroring config.json). WHY THIS EXISTS RATHER THAN A BARE `int` CAST. Backend field values arrive as strings, and Hugo's `int` is FATAL on a malformed one — even "5 " with a trailing space (verified empirically on 0.158). The counts this validates are read by index.html, where any fault kills the WHOLE home node: the homepage AND both feeds. A typo in the settings form could therefore have taken the site down on the next full rebuild. So: never cast raw input. Accept a digits-only value, otherwise keep the fallback (which also covers blank and missing params). Leading zeros are rejected on purpose — Go parses "010" as OCTAL, so `int` would return 8 (verified). Guard verified on 0.91.2, 0.158 and 0.163 with the matrix "8"->8, 7->7, "5 "/""/"abc"/"010"/missing->fallback. */}} {{- $value := .fallback -}} {{- with findRE `^(0|[1-9][0-9]*)$` (trim (printf "%v" .raw) " ") 1 -}} {{- $value = int (index . 0) -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $value -}}
